VIP customers are identified by lifetime ride count over 500, lifetime spend over 50,000 INR, or corporate account status. VIP tickets receive priority routing: P1-P2 tickets bypass Tier 1 and go directly to Tier 2 specialists. VIPs have a dedicated phone line staffed by senior agents 8 AM to 10 PM IST. SLA targets for VIPs are reduced by 50 percent: P1 response in 7 minutes, P2 in 30 minutes.
