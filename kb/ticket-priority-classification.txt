Ola classifies support tickets into four priority levels: P1 (Critical), P2 (High), P3 (Medium), and P4 (Low). P1 tickets involve complete service outages or safety incidents requiring immediate response within 15 minutes. P2 tickets cover major feature failures or payment errors with a 1-hour response target. P3 and P4 tickets cover single-user issues and general inquiries with 4-hour and 24-hour response windows respectively.
