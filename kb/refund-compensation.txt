Ola provides refunds and compensation based on incident type and customer impact. For ride cancellations due to driver unavailability, riders receive a full fare refund plus a 50 INR goodwill credit within 24 hours. For overcharging or billing errors, the excess amount is refunded to the original payment method within 3-5 business days. Service outages lasting more than 30 minutes qualify affected riders for a 100 INR ride credit and drivers for a 200 INR earnings protection payment.
