The escalation matrix defines four tiers of escalation paths based on ticket severity and time elapsed. Tier 1 frontline agents handle all incoming tickets and can resolve P3-P4 independently. Tier 2 senior specialists receive P2 tickets or P3/P4 tickets unresolved after 50% of SLA time. Tier 3 engineering leads are engaged for P1 incidents or any ticket exceeding 75% of its SLA without resolution.
